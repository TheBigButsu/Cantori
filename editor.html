Formulae & stat reference — how the numbers actually work

Combat

Hit chance50% + (acc − eva) × 3%, clamped to 10%–95% (even acc/eva = 50%; each point of accuracy over evasion = +3%). A target that hasn't noticed you is auto-hit for ×1.5 damage (surprise).
Your accuracybase + DEX + weapon acc + level acc
Your evasionbase + DEX + level eva + armor subtype (light +2, medium 0, heavy −3)
Damage dealtroll(dmgMin..dmgMax) + STR bonus + bonuses − armor block − VIT resist, minimum 1. Armor block is itself a fresh roll of defMin..defMax each hit, so heavier armor absorbs a variable amount.
Critical hitBase 5% chance for 200% damage, each raised by the class's per-level crit / critDmg gains (added, not multiplied).
Attack cost (time)1 / (weapon speed × (1 + haste)) — lower is faster. Monsters bank speed energy per turn and act once per whole point.
Weapon range1 = melee (adjacent). 2+ = tap a monster within reach + line of sight to strike (spear 2, bow 5).

Enchants (proc rolls per hit at proc)

burnpower × burstMult now, then a single burning stack (dotTurns). Refreshes — never stacks.
poisoninitial hit now, then perTurn for turns. Every proc adds an independent stack.
shockpower × burstMult, stun chance = burst × stunPer / target level.
thornswhen you're struck, reflect power × mult back at the attacker.
hastepassive while worn — folds into attack speed above (mult).
defensepassive while worn — adds amount flat mitigation to every block. Rolls on armor / rings / necklaces.
power =weapon top-end damage (on your strike) · armor def (thorns/retaliation) · tier + plus (jewelry)

Progression

Max HPbaseHp + VIT (1 VIT = 1 HP; baseHp defaults to 13), plus the class levelUp hp gained per level.
HP regenHeals to full over the class's regenTurns, sped up by Vitality: effective = regenTurns − VIT × vitRegen. Per-turn heal = maxHP / effective (default 600 turns, factor 2).
MP regenSame shape, sped up by Intelligence: effective = mpRegenTurns − INT × intRegen, per-turn = maxMP / effective (default 600, factor 2).
Identifyneed = (tier + plus) × (rand 1..10 + rarity rank); +1 progress each time you use the item (strike / get hit).
XP to levellevel × 8. On level: main stat +2, secondary +1, +1 free point, plus the class levelUp set (hp/mp/acc/eva).
Monster XPceil(minFloor / 2) — floor 1–2 = 1, floor 3–4 = 2, floor 5 = 3. Bosses give a larger scaled reward.
